Thank You: Message Received, Partner

Every form on this site — contact, applications, reports, submissions — lands here when it succeeds, so this page explains the pattern once: your message is in, it has a reference, and each form type has a stated response window that starts now.

What happens next

Support and problem reports: first human reply within one business day, usually faster. Applications (wholesale, supplier, affiliate, careers): the timelines printed on their own pages apply, and silence past the deadline is itself reportable through Report a Problem. Submissions: reading is human and weekly-batched for the community walls.

Your reference

The confirmation shows a reference code — keep it. Quoting it in any follow-up pulls your message instantly instead of restarting the story. If this page appeared without a reference (a glitch), tell us through a second route and the glitch itself gets a ticket.

Didn't mean to send?

Forms here do not trigger purchases, subscriptions or anything irreversible — the worst a stray submission does is start a conversation. Reply to the confirmation email or message contact and it closes cleanly.
