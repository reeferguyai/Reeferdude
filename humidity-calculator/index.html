Humidity & Drying Calculator: Hold The Numbers

Humidity is the invisible variable that decides mold, growth rate and cure quality. This calculator handles its two big jobs: VPD (vapor pressure deficit) for the living grow — the number that tells you if your temperature and humidity combination is actually in range — and dry-room tracking for harvest, including how much water weight your crop will lose.

VPD without tears

Enter temperature and relative humidity; get your VPD and the stage verdict — seedling, veg, flower each want a different band. The guide text under the result explains in one paragraph why VPD matters (it is the plant's drinking pressure) and what to adjust first: temperature is usually cheaper to move than humidity.

Drying math

Wet harvest weight in, expected dry weight range out (typically 20–25% of wet), plus the jar humidity targets for cure. The harvest guide's 60/60 rule lives here as a live calculator so you can check your actual room against it hour by hour.

Demo note

Like all tools here, the calculator runs entirely in your browser once live — no data leaves the page. This demonstration publishes the full tool design and formulas for inspection.
