Harm Reduction: Safe Use, Straight Up

Harm reduction starts from respect: adults will make their choices, and honest information makes those choices safer. This page is the shop's core safety document — dosing, combinations to avoid, impairment, bad-experience protocols, and when to get real help.

Dose discipline

Inhaled: one hit, wait ten minutes, then decide. Edibles: 2.5–5mg THC for beginners, wait a full two hours — the classic edible injury is impatience, stacking a second dose on a first that has not landed. Concentrates are measured in rice grains, not globs. The dosing calculator turns your product and tolerance into a sane starting number.

Mixing and impairment

Alcohol plus cannabis multiplies impairment unpredictably — the crossfade is a warning, not a feature. Never drive after use. Some medications interact with cannabinoids (the cannabinoid pages flag the known ones); if you take prescriptions, that conversation belongs with your clinician, and this page will not pretend otherwise.

Bad nights and real help

Too much, too fast: get somewhere safe and comfortable, hydrate, eat, rest — it passes, usually within hours for smoked, longer for edibles. Chest pain, severe confusion, or a person who cannot be roused is a call-emergency-services event, not a ride-it-out event. Looking out for each other is porch law.
