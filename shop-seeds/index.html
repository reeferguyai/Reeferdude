Seeds: Where Every Grow Starts

Seeds are genetics in waiting — and in many places they are sold as souvenirs or for legal home cultivation only. This category is education-first: how seeds work, how to store them, what germination takes, plus the germination and storage gear that is legal to ship anywhere.

Know your law first

Cannabis seed legality varies by country, state and even city, and home-grow rules change often. Nothing on this page is legal advice. Check your local rules before buying seeds anywhere — our legal guide by region is the starting point, not the answer.

What makes a good seed

Healthy seeds are firm, dark-streaked and waxy; pale, soft or cracked seeds rarely germinate. Breeder, lineage and testing matter more than hype names — the strain library explains what to look for in a description.

Gear we can ship anywhere

Germination kits, humidity packs and seed storage vaults are ordinary gardening supplies. Those are the products listed here, alongside the seed buying guide and germination guide in the Learn section.
