Leaderboards: The Porch Rankings

Leaderboards rank the porch's play: Puff Quest XP, challenge completions, quiz high scores and community contribution points. They reset seasonally, they reward showing up over spending, and they are strictly opt-in — compete loudly or not at all, the porch respects both.

What counts

Learning counts most: quizzes passed, guides finished, challenges completed. Community counts next: stories published, photos featured, helpful answers. Purchases count least and cap early — a leaderboard money can buy is a receipt, not a ranking, and this one refuses to be one.

Seasons and resets

Boards reset each season with the champions archived on the shoutouts page — last season's king returns to zero with the rest of us, which is exactly what keeps the game kind. Lifetime totals live privately on your account for your own satisfaction.

Fair play

Multi-account farming, botted quizzes and submitted-work theft (claiming another's photo or story) end accounts' board eligibility permanently. The community rules page has the enforcement ladder; it is short because violations are rare and obvious.
