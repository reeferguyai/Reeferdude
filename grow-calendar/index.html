Grow Calendar

The grow calendar maps the year for hemp growers: what to do each month, indoors and outdoors. Adjust timing for your climate, since seasons shift by region, and use the grow guides for the details behind each task.

Winter: plan and prepare

Winter is for planning: choose strains, review last season notes, service equipment, and start indoor grows where space allows. Order supplies early so spring never waits on shipping.

Spring and summer: grow

Spring starts outdoor preparation as frost risk passes, and summer is active growth: watering, feeding, training, and pest checks. Watch plants daily in heat, and keep a log so next season starts smarter.

Autumn: harvest and cure

Autumn brings harvest timing, careful drying, and slow curing. After harvest, clean the space, store equipment, and write the season notes that make next winter planning session useful.
