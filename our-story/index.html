Our Story: Mountain Roots, Kush Dreams

Every shop has an origin story; ours is a workbench. A solo device-repair technician with decades of hands-on tech work and a sales streak started sketching a different kind of hemp shop — one run like a porch, not a warehouse — and built it piece by piece in public.

The short timeline

2024–2025: the domains are gathered and the GanjaGuru blueprint is drawn — a cosmic, AI-guided hemp universe with a budtender at its heart. 2026: the storefront takes shape — flagship site, store engine, dozens of pages of guides — built openly, fixed openly, with the mistakes left in the changelog where they belong.

Why a porch

A porch is where knowledge passes hand to hand: how to roll, how to grow, what to avoid, who makes good glass. The shop is designed as that porch at internet scale — the budtender talks like a neighbor because that is the entire point.

Where it is going

The roadmap runs through live commerce with hemp-aware payments, a maker marketplace with real independent glass, and the gamified learning world of the blueprint — Budz, XP, challenges — layered in as each piece earns its place.
