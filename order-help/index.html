Orders & Customer Help: Real Answers

This is the front door for order questions: how ordering works, how to change or cancel, where tracking lives, and how to reach a person when the pages do not cover your case. Start here before emailing — most answers are one paragraph each.

Before you ask

Three pages solve most order questions: Track a Delivery for where your package is, Returns & Exchanges for sending something back, and Order History for what you bought and when. Each is linked at the bottom of this page.

Demo storefront note

This shop is a demonstration build: checkout is not live, no orders are being taken and no payments are processed. These help pages describe exactly how ordering will work at launch, so there are no surprises when it opens.

Reaching a human

When the pages fail you, the contact-by-topic page routes your message — sales, support, press and wholesale each land with the right person instead of one overflowing inbox.
