Bulk & Corporate Orders: Volume, Handled Straight

Weddings with custom etched glass, hotels stocking amenities within the law, companies ordering branded gear for events — bulk is its own machine: a quote, a proof, a production run, a delivery date in writing. This page starts it.

How quoting works

Tell us items, quantities, customisation and your date. Quotes return within two business days with per-unit pricing at your volume, production time, and the honest rush options if your date is tight. Custom branding follows the brand assets rules for our marks and the print file specs for yours.

Minimums and reality

Bulk pricing starts where it makes sense per product — the quote says the threshold instead of hiding it. Production runs of custom work cannot be cancelled once proofed and in production; the cancellation policy states the exact stages, in plain language.

Corporate responsibility line

We do not supply products for resale into jurisdictions where they are illegal, and corporate gift orders carry the same age rules as everything else here. The compliance team reviews bulk orders as a matter of routine — it protects your event as much as our licence.
