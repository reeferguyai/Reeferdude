Out Of Stock & Back-In-Stock Alerts

When a piece sells out, this page is where its return gets announced — and where you sign up to hear about it first. Some items restock on a cycle; some are one-run pieces from independent makers and never return. Each product page says which kind it is, honestly.

How alerts work

Enter your email on any out-of-stock product and you get exactly one message when it is back — no newsletter signup smuggled in, no marketing after. If the item is retired instead, you get one message saying so, with the closest alternative.

Demo note

The storefront is in demonstration mode, so alert signups show the intended flow rather than storing live subscriptions yet. The wishlist page offers the same watch function inside this demo.

Makers' runs

Independent makers work in small runs. A sold-out maker piece may return in a different color or form — the maker's page on Brands & Makers tracks their current work.
