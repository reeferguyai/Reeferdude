Submit: Your Strain, Photo, Story Or Design

Everything the community builds here comes through this door: a strain we have not catalogued, a photo for the gallery, a story for the shelves, a design for the Studio wall. This page explains what to send for each kind, what happens next, and the rights you keep — all of them.

What to send

Strains: name, lineage if known, where you encountered it, your notes on effects and growth — photos help identification. Photos: original files, your credit name, one line of story. Stories: your words, any length, with privacy instructions if names need changing. Designs: through the Studio, under its own submission flow linked below.

What happens next

A human reads every submission. Strains get researched against the library's sources before listing — community strains are labelled as community-reported until verified. Photos and stories are lightly edited for length and clarity only, with changes shown to you before anything posts.

The rights, again

Yours. Submission grants display here with credit, nothing more, revocable with one message. The IP policy has the legal wording; this page has the promise, and they match.
