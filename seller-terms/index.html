Seller Terms

These terms govern selling on the ReeferDude marketplace. Sellers agree to them when they list, and buyers rely on them for every order. Hemp-derived products only: no actual cannabis may be listed, ever.

Listing honesty

Listings must describe products accurately, including contents, materials, and origin. Lab results must back tested-product claims, and photos must show the actual product. Misleading listings are removed, and repeat problems end selling privileges.

Fulfillment

Sellers ship within the timeframe shown on the listing and provide tracking through the store engine. Print-on-demand sellers follow production timelines stated at listing time. Delays must be communicated to buyers promptly through the order system.

Fees and enforcement

Marketplace fees and payout terms are confirmed when a seller account is approved, since they depend on category and volume. The marketplace may hold payouts during disputes, remove listings that break these terms, and close seller accounts for serious or repeated violations.
