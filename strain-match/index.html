Strain Match By Mood: Tell Me The Evening

Sometimes you do not know a strain; you know an evening. Mood match starts there: pick the feeling you are after and the tool lines up the strains whose terpene and effect profiles point that way — each with the honesty note that mood is chemistry plus company, and company does half the work.

The moods

Focus & Flow (pinene and limonene-leaning profiles), Laughter & Company (the classic social hybrids), Deep Couch (myrcene-heavy, evening-only), Create Something (the bright sativa-leaning classics), and Sunset Porch (balanced hybrids, the house specialty). Each mood page section explains its terpene logic in plain words.

Built on the library

Matches pull live from the strain library's data, so as the library grows the matches improve. Every suggestion carries its potency band — a mood match that ignores your tolerance is a prank, not a tool.

Save your moods

Members can save a mood board of matched strains against future evenings. In this demonstration build, the full matching logic and data are published for inspection.
