Custom: Made On The Porch

Custom is where the catalog meets the Studio: glass etched with your design, rolling trays printed with your art, and one-off pieces made to order. Everything here starts as a design — yours or ours — and ends as gear nobody else on earth owns.

How custom orders work

Pick the base piece, open it in the Design Studio, place your art or text, and preview it in 3D before committing. Because custom pieces are made to order, they take longer than shelf stock — each listing states its honest lead time.

Your art, your rights

You keep full rights to anything you upload. We only produce the piece you ordered. The intellectual property policy spells this out in full, and user-submitted designs are never sold to anyone else without a separate licence from you.

Start from a template

Not starting from zero? The Studio templates page has porch-tested layouts — names, dates, strain art, memorial pieces — that you can personalise in minutes.
