Meet The Crew: The Porch Is People

A shop is its people. This page introduces the crew: the founders who built the place, the budtender voice you talk to, the makers whose glass fills the shelves, and the support humans who answer when something goes sideways.

Founders

The venture is a family build — founded by Ray Meadows and Jessica Mullins, whose names sit on the domain records and the owner stamp in the site's DNS. The mission they set is the one on the wall: build the front porch of hemp culture, and build it to last for the next generation.

The budtender

Ray the AI budtender — the Ganjagoddessai voice — is the shop's always-awake crew member: strain questions, gear advice, grow basics, at 3 PM or 3 AM. It is an AI and says so; anything it cannot answer routes to a human through contact by topic.

Makers & support

Independent glassblowers and gear makers stock the shelves through the marketplace, and a small support crew reads every Report a Problem message personally. No ticket bots, no scripts that pretend to be people.
