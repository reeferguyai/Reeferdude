Partners

ReeferDude grows through partners: suppliers, makers, creators, and retailers who share the brand commitment to honest hemp commerce. This page covers the kinds of partnerships available and how to start one.

Supplier partners

Suppliers provide hemp-derived products, materials, and components for the store and marketplace. Testing, compliance, and labeling standards apply to every supplier product, with lab results backing claims where testing applies.

Maker and creator partners

Makers sell custom and handmade goods through the marketplace and design studio. Creators publish guides, videos, and courses with the brand, reaching the community through content rather than ads alone.

Retail partners

Retailers stock ReeferDude products and bundles in their own shops, under wholesale terms. Retail partnership inquiries go through the contact page with business details and expected volumes.
