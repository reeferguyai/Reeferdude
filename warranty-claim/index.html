Warranty Claim: We Stand By Our Stuff

Gear fails sometimes: a joint cracks, a vape battery dies young, a grinder tooth snaps. If it failed doing what it was made to do, that is on us. This page starts the claim — order number, item, what happened, a photo if you can — and most claims resolve within two business days.

Covered and not

Covered: manufacturing defects, dead-on-arrival electronics, seams and joints that fail in normal use, within the warranty window on the product page. Not covered: drops, thermal shock from boiling or freezing glass, misuse, and wear items like screens and coils used past their life.

How it resolves

Approved claims get a replacement shipped or a refund to the original payment — your choice on most items. You will rarely be asked to ship the broken piece back; a photo usually settles it.
