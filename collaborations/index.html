Collaborations & Custom Drops: Built Together

The best pieces in the catalog's future are collaborations: a glassblower's limited run, an artist's tray series, a creator's signature grinder. This page explains how collaborations are proposed, how revenue and credit are structured, and where drops get announced.

How a collab starts

Either direction — we approach makers whose work fits, makers pitch through the supplier application with 'collaboration' flagged. Every collab is scoped in writing first: quantities, pricing, revenue split, who owns the design (the maker, always, unless separately licensed), and what happens to unsold stock.

Drops, run fairly

Limited drops announce to the newsletter first with a stated time, one-per-customer on the scarcest pieces, and no bot-bait mechanics. Sold-out drops publish their numbers afterward — how many existed is a fact buyers deserve.

Credit as currency

Collaborators are named everywhere the work appears — product page, gallery, press. The shop's name never appears larger than the maker's on their own piece. That rule is why makers say yes.
