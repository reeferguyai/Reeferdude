Advertise With Us

ReeferDude reaches people who are actively learning about hemp: growers, makers, and buyers reading guides and using tools. Advertising here puts a relevant brand in front of that audience, in clearly labeled placements.

Where ads appear

  • Site placements across guides, the blog, and tools pages.
  • Newsletter placements in the regular subscriber digest.
  • Community and event sponsorships, labeled as sponsored content.

What we accept

We accept advertising that fits a hemp learning audience and follows the law, including hemp compliance rules and platform ad policies. Ads are always labeled, never disguised as editorial content, and never presented as endorsements unless a partnership says so explicitly.

How to inquire

Send your brand, product category, and placement interest through the contact page. Include your timeline and budget range so the reply can be specific. Rates and availability are confirmed per inquiry, since they depend on placement and season.
