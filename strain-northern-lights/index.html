Northern Lights

Northern Lights is one of the most requested cultivars in the library. This page is its full record: genetics and kind, typical potency, the effects the community reports, its flavor and terpene signature, and what growers should expect.

The profile

Northern Lights is a indica cultivar testing around 16–21% THC. Its reported effects run sleepy, calm, pain-quieting — read them as community consensus, not a promise: set, setting and your own chemistry edit every session.

Flavor & aroma

The flavor leads sweet, earthy, spice. The dominant terpenes — myrcene, caryophyllene, bisabolol — explain most of that profile; the terpene pages in the library break down what each one contributes.

Growing it

Easy — the beginner's indica, resilient. The stage guides (seedling through harvest) cover the full run, and the troubleshooting guide handles the problems this cultivar is known to invite.
