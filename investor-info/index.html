Investor Information: The Business, Plainly

This page is the front door for investment interest, written the way the shop writes everything: the model in plain terms, the stage stated honestly, the risks named alongside the opportunity, and the route to a real conversation.

The model

Three engines, one porch: commerce (gear and custom work through the store), services (design studio, print-on-demand, consultations), and audience (content, community and the AI budtender as the always-on front door). The portfolio strategy pairs a flagship brand with a dedicated store domain so each does the job it is best at.

The stage, honestly

Early. The storefront is in demonstration build with commerce staged to open on hemp-aware payment rails; the audience assets (Local Guide reach near ten million views, community channels) predate the shop and are its unfair advantage. Revenue today is nascent — anyone telling you otherwise about a business at this stage is rehearsing, not reporting.

Start the conversation

Serious enquiries through this page get the data room: traffic, costs, the regulatory analysis including the December 2026 federal changes, and the roadmap. The porch raises on fit and patience, not on hype cycles.
