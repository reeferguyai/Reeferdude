Caryophyllene

Caryophyllene is one of the major cannabis terpenes — the aromatic compounds that give strains their smell and much of their character. This page is its full profile.

The aroma

Caryophyllene smells like spicy black pepper, warm wood. It also lives in black pepper, cloves, hops, cinnamon — which is why a strain can smell like a pine forest or a lemon grove: the same molecules, in a different plant.

What it does in a profile

The only terpene that also acts as a cannabinoid, binding CB2 receptors directly. Peppery strains often feel steadier and less racy — caryophyllene is the profile's anchor.

Finding it

Strains in this library where it leads: Blue Dream, OG Kush, Sour Diesel.
