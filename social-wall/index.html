Social Wall: The Porch, Out In The World

The conversation also happens out there — marketplace posts, community photos, short videos, announcements. This wall gathers the shop's public social posts into one chronological stream on our own site, readable without an account on any platform and without the platforms' tracking coming along for the ride.

Why host our own wall

Social platforms rent you your own audience back. The wall keeps the record home: posts mirrored here with dates intact, so a platform's whim cannot erase the community's history — the same reasoning as the newsletter archive, applied to the feed.

What appears

The shop's official posts across its channels, newest first, with platform and date attached. Community members appear only where they posted publicly to the shop's pages or tagged the shop — and any member can have a mirrored post removed with one message, no argument.

Follow at the source

The links page holds the canonical profiles. Follow there if you live there; read here if you do not — either way you see the same porch.
