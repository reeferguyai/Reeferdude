Takedown & Complaints: The Fast Door For Rights Holders

If your copyrighted work is on this site without permission, this page is the shortest route to its removal — written for humans, compliant with the DMCA's requirements, and honest about timelines and the counter-notice process that protects posters from wrongful removal.

What to send

Your name and contact, identification of your work, the exact URL of the infringing copy here, a good-faith statement, and your signature (typed is fine) under penalty of perjury that you own the work or act for its owner. Send it via this page's form or the copyright contact in the site footer — incomplete notices get a reply listing what is missing, not a shrug.

What happens

Valid notices: removal within two business days, the poster notified with the notice's contents, and the poster's counter-notice route explained to them in the same message. Counter-noticed content is restored in 10–14 business days unless the claimant files in court — the statutory dance, danced exactly.

Abuse of the door

Takedown is a legal instrument, not a dislike button. Knowingly false notices carry legal exposure for the filer and get published (redacted) in the changelog when they target community work — sunlight being the porch's favorite disinfectant.
