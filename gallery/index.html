Art & Photo Gallery: The Culture In Pictures

The gallery is the culture with the words stripped off: reader-submitted photography and artwork of grows at golden hour, glass in sunlight, trim tables, porches, and the long strange history of the plant in images. Every piece is credited; every submission is a small act of trust we take seriously.

What gets shown

Original work only, submitted by its maker through the submit page, with the maker's name and story attached. Grow photos must be from legal grows — the gallery does not launder evidence, and location data is stripped from every file before posting.

Rights stay yours

Submitting shows your work here with credit; it does not transfer ownership, and takedown is one message away, forever. The user-submitted designs page runs the same rules for Studio art — the policy is the culture: makers own their work.

The standing collections

Glass In Sunlight (the makers' gallery), Golden Hour Grows, Porches of the World, and the historical wall — public-domain imagery from the history timeline, hemp's industrial century included.
