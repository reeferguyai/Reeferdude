Data Retention: How Long Things Are Kept

Privacy policies say 'we may retain data'; this page says how long, in a table you can hold us to. Every data class the shop touches is listed with its retention clock, the reason, and what happens when the clock runs out.

The schedule

Accounts: life of the account plus 30 days after deletion request. Orders and invoices: 7 years (tax law), isolated from marketing systems after year one. Support messages: 3 years. Analytics: aggregated at 14 months, raw events gone by 26 months. Design files: yours, kept while your account lives, exportable anytime, deleted with the account. Contest and challenge records: the season plus one year.

Why anything is kept

Each clock traces to a reason printed beside it — law, warranty, fraud prevention, or the feature itself (your saved designs cannot exist without storage). Data with no remaining reason is deleted on schedule, not 'reviewed periodically'. Backups age out within 90 days of any deletion, so erased means erased everywhere within a quarter.

Your controls

The privacy requests page is the working door: see it, export it, delete it. Deletion here is deletion — the only carve-outs are the legal-retention rows above, isolated and named.
