Maintenance: The Porch Is Being Swept

If you are reading this in the wild, the site is briefly down for planned work — upgrades, migrations, the occasional deep clean. This page exists so the downtime is explained instead of mysterious: what is happening, how long it should take, and where live updates post.

Planned means planned

Maintenance windows are announced on the status page and the newsletter at least 48 hours ahead, scheduled for the quietest hours we have, and kept to their stated length or publicly extended with a reason. Unplanned outages wear a different page and a franker tone.

Your stuff is safe

Accounts, orders, designs and data are not touched by display maintenance; when work does touch data, it is backed up first and the changelog records what changed. A maintenance page is a curtain, not a demolition.

While you wait

The budtender chat and the status page run on separate rails and usually stay up through maintenance. The community, being a community, will be on the social channels doing what porches do when the lights blink.
