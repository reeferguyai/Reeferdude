Fact-Check & Sources: Show Your Work

Trust is a verification process, not a vibe. This page documents how the site's factual claims — legal summaries, health-adjacent education, product specs, historical content — are sourced, what evidence classes we accept, and how corrections happen in public.

Evidence classes

Class A: primary sources — statutes, official regulators, peer-reviewed studies. Class B: reputable secondary reporting and industry data. Class C: community consensus and tradition, always labelled as such. Legal and safety claims require Class A; product specs require measurement or manufacturer documentation; culture content may ride on Class C with the label showing.

Corrections policy

Errors reported through Report a Problem are checked against sources and fixed with a changelog entry naming what changed and why. Legal pages carry check dates and get re-verified on a cycle and whenever a reader flags a change — law moves, and a stale legal page is worse than none.

Challenge us

Every graded claim on the myths and facts page, every state summary, every guide number is open to sourced challenge. Bring a better source and the page improves; the challenger gets credited in the changelog. That offer is permanent.
