Saved Addresses: Your Doors, Remembered

Checkout is faster when the doors you use are already written down. This page manages your saved addresses: add, edit, set a default, and note which ones can take an adult-signature delivery — the detail that saves a missed box.

Signature-ready flags

Each address can be flagged 'adult usually present' or 'depot pickup preferred'. That flag travels with age-restricted orders so the carrier plan matches your real life instead of a guess.

Demo note

Accounts run in preview mode on this demonstration storefront; address saving shows the production design and stores nothing live yet.

Your data, your call

Addresses are account data like everything else here — exportable and deletable under the privacy requests page, and never shared with anyone but the carrier printing your label.
