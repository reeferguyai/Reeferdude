Dealer & Retailer Program: The Porch In Your Shop

Beyond wholesale pricing sits the dealer program: shops that feature the line properly — a display corner, staff who can talk terpenes, the brand assets used right — in exchange for territory consideration, co-op marketing and first access to limited maker runs.

What dealers commit

A real display (the kit is in the application), staff completion of the free budtending course (two evenings, online), and the same honesty standards the shop holds itself: our values page is a contract clause here, not a poster.

What dealers get

Territory consideration for the full line, co-op funds matched against local promotion, maker-run allocations that never reach the public catalog, and listing on the store location page's dealer map when it launches.

Fit check

The program suits independent smoke shops, hemp retailers and culture stores who already run their floor like a porch. Big-box enquiries go to bulk and corporate orders instead — different machine, same honesty.
