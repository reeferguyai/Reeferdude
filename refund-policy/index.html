Refund Policy

Refunds follow the situation: damaged, defective, and incorrect orders are refunded or replaced, while change-of-mind returns follow the shipping and returns rules. This page covers the money side; the shipping page covers the logistics.

When refunds apply

Refunds apply when an order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, and for returns accepted under the shipping and returns policy. Personalized and custom-made pieces are refunded when they arrive damaged or defective, not for change of mind.

How refunds are processed

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through the store engine. Processing time depends on the payment provider, and you will receive confirmation when the refund is issued.

How to request a refund

Contact us through the contact page with the order number, what arrived, and what is wrong. Photos help for damaged items. Digital product refunds follow the same process, with the rules for already-downloaded products explained on the digital products page.
