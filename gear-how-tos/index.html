Bong, Pipe, Vape & Grinder How-Tos

Every tool in the smoking kit has a right way that nobody explains at the shop counter. This hub gathers the four core how-tos — bong, pipe, vape, grinder — each covering loading, technique, and the maintenance that keeps the tool at its best.

Bong how-to

Water to just above the downstem slits, grind medium, pack the bowl with a light tamp, corner the bowl with the flame to stretch it across multiple hits, and clear the chamber fully each pull. Change the water every session — bong water is not a beverage and old water is a biofilm with ambitions.

Vape & grinder how-to

Vapes: grind fine for conduction, medium for convection, pack to the maker's fill line, start low and step the temperature up through the bowl. Grinders: do not overfill, turn against resistance in both directions, and tap — don't bang — the kief loose. The individual guides linked below go deep on each tool.

Pipe how-to

The humble spoon: pack in layers (loose, medium, firm on top — the 'truffle' method), light the edge, sip rather than rip. A pipe smoked slowly tastes like a different, better plant than the same flower torched in three pulls.
