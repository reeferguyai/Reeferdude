Soil, Hydro & Light: The Three Foundations

Every grow stands on three choices: what the roots live in, what the leaves eat light from, and how the two are managed together. This combined guide compares soil, coco and hydro honestly, then matches each to the lighting that makes it sing — so the foundation decision gets made once, well.

Mediums compared

Soil forgives: it buffers pH and feeding mistakes, grows the tastiest flower, yields a touch less. Coco coir is hydro's friendly cousin — more control, more feeding work, faster growth. Full hydro (DWC, flood tables) is the fastest and the least forgiving: a pump failure is a funeral. New growers belong in soil or coco, full stop.

Lighting reality

LEDs won: full-spectrum LED fixtures deliver the best grams per watt with the least heat, and the grow lights page catalogs them. What matters is delivered light (PPFD) at the canopy, not the wattage printed in the biggest font — a light map beats a headline number every time.

Putting it together

Budget split that works: light first (40%), environment control second (30%), medium and nutrients third (30%). Growers upgrade everything except the light and wonder why nothing improves. The grow cost calculator models your exact tent before you spend a dollar.
