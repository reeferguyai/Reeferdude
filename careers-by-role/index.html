Careers By Role: Work On The Porch

The shop hires slowly and keeps people. This page organises opportunities by role family — bench and fulfilment, makers and studio, budtender support and content, tech — with the honest shape of each job: the work, the setting, and who thrives in it.

The role families

Bench & Fulfilment: testing, packing, quality — hands-on, in-person in Franklin, Ohio. Studio & Makers: design production, print and glass partnerships — portfolio over pedigree. Voice & Content: the budtender's human editors, guide writers, community moderators. Tech: the storefront, the tools, the AI — small team, whole stack.

How hiring works

Postings appear here and on the main careers page with real salary bands — no 'competitive' fog. Applications are read by the person you would work for. Every applicant hears back; silence is not a status we use.

The standing door

No fitting posting? The general application on the careers page stays open for the right people — several porch chairs began exactly that way. Device-repair experience, by the way, is family here: the founder's bench is the original.
