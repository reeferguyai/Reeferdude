Cancellation Policy: Changing Your Mind, Fairly

Cancellations are where shops show their character, so the rules are published stage by stage: what can be cancelled, until when, what it costs (usually nothing), and the few places where work already done changes the answer.

Shelf orders

Any unshipped shelf order cancels in full, instantly, from the order page or one message — no reason required, no retention script. Once shipped, the returns process takes over with the same refund result; the distinction is logistics, not generosity.

Custom & bulk work

Custom pieces cancel free until production begins (the proof approval is the line), at material cost during production, and are non-cancellable once finished — they are, literally, made only for you. Bulk orders follow the same stages at their scale; the bulk page's quote restates them per order.

Subscriptions & memberships

Memberships cancel anytime from the account page, effective at the end of the paid period, with perks usable until then. No cancellation flows designed to exhaust you — one page, one confirmation, done. The subscription terms carry the legal wording.
