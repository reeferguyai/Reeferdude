Payment Options: How Paying Will Work

Payment is the least glamorous and most important page in a hemp shop, because this industry does not get the easy options. This page states plainly which payment routes the store is built around and why the familiar logos are not among them.

Why not the usual buttons

Mainstream processors restrict or close hemp and CBD businesses — accounts get frozen, not warned. The store is built on hemp-aware payment gateways that underwrite this industry openly: card payments through those gateways, plus bank transfer options at launch.

Security promises

Card details are handled by the payment gateway on its own secure pages — this site never sees or stores your card number. The Security page describes the full setup, and the payment terms in Legal spell out refunds, chargebacks and disputes.

Demo note

Checkout is not live on this demonstration storefront, so no payment of any kind is currently accepted or requested. Any page asking you to pay here today is not us — report it via Report a Problem.
