Franchise & Licensing: Take The Porch To Your Town

Franchising here means licensing a whole way of operating — the brand, the catalog relationships, the budtender tooling, the honesty standards — for a territory you run as your own business. This enquiry page starts the fit conversation; nothing here is an offer document, and the formal disclosures come through the proper legal channel before any money moves.

What licenses well

The model suits culture-first retailers in legal markets: a physical porch (shop or lounge-adjacent retail) with the digital storefront and AI budtender as its always-open extension. The dealer program is the lighter first step — many franchise conversations start there and graduate.

What we protect

The brand standards are the licence's core: the values, the testing gauntlet, the community rules. A franchisee who cuts those corners loses the sign — that clause is why the sign is worth having, and it is not negotiable in either direction.

Timeline honesty

The franchise program formalises after the flagship's commerce is proven live. Enquiries now join the founding conversation and the waitlist — early, informed, and without a dollar requested until the legal pack exists.
