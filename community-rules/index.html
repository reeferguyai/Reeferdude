Community Rules: Peace First, Always

The porch is friendly because it is governed, lightly and in the open. These are the whole rules — short enough to actually read — covering posts, photos, stories, comments and the marketplace, plus exactly how reporting and enforcement work when someone breaks them.

The rules

One: be kind — argue with ideas, never people. Two: be legal — no content facilitating illegal activity, no evidence-posting, no sales outside the marketplace's rules. Three: be honest — your work, your photos, your experience level. Four: be safe — harm reduction is the house position; content that endangers people goes. Five: respect privacy — nobody's real name, address or business appears here without their consent.

Reporting

Every post, photo, story and listing carries a report route; reports go to human moderators, and the reporter hears the outcome. Anonymous reporting is fully supported — the porch protects the people who protect the porch.

Enforcement ladder

Reminder, warning, content removal, posting pause, removal from the community — in that order, with appeals answered by a different moderator than the one who acted. The ladder is published so nobody has to guess where they stand, including us.
