Media Enquiries: Talk To The Porch

Journalists, podcasters, documentary makers: this door is yours. Interview requests, background questions, fact-checking our claims (encouraged — the fact-check page is the standing invitation), and requests for data or access that the press kit does not cover.

What we say yes to

Interviews about the business, the build-in-public story, hemp industry realities, AI in small retail, and the legal landscape as a small operator lives it. We talk plainly, including about what is not working — a press relationship built on varnish is worth nothing to either side.

Speed

Flag your deadline in the first line. Same-day turnaround on factual questions is the norm; interviews schedule within the week. Embargoes are honored when agreed in writing before information changes hands.

Not this door

Customer support belongs to contact by topic (it is monitored faster for orders), and creator partnerships belong to the influencer program. Press releases and announcements live on the press page.
