Travel & Cannabis: Borders Are Serious

The fastest way to ruin a trip is assuming your home rules packed themselves in your bag. This page covers the hard lines: aircraft and borders, state-line crossings, national parks, hotels and rentals — written plainly because the penalties are not.

The iron rules

Never cross an international border with cannabis in any form — not even between two legal places (US–Canada included): border law is federal, penalties are severe, and 'I forgot it was in my bag' is a confession, not a defense. Flying domestically in the US: TSA is not hunting your stash, but federal law governs airports and aircraft, and discovery can end trips and worse.

Driving and state lines

Crossing from a legal state into a prohibition state with product converts your legal purchase into contraband at the line. Impaired driving is illegal everywhere and the science of roadside testing lags the politics — the only safe rule is no driving after use, full stop, covered properly in harm reduction.

Lodging and parks

Hotels and rentals can ban smoking and vaping on property regardless of state law — check before you light, and expect cleaning fees enforced with enthusiasm. National parks and all federal land follow federal law: prohibition applies even inside legal states.
