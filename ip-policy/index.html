Intellectual Property: Yours Stays Yours

The shop runs on other people's creativity — makers' glass, artists' designs, the community's photos — so its IP rules are load-bearing: what belongs to whom, what licences the shop takes (narrow ones), and what it never claims (your work).

The core rule

Uploading, submitting or designing here transfers nothing. Makers and members keep full ownership of their work; the shop takes a display-and-produce licence limited to what you asked for — showing your gallery piece, producing your custom order, displaying your marketplace listing. Licences end when you remove the work, except for pieces already produced and delivered.

Trademarks & brand

The shop's names and logos are its trademarks; partners use them under the brand assets rules. We respect others' marks the same way — no strain-name or brand usage here implies affiliation with any breeder or company beyond stated partnerships.

Infringement claims

If your work appears here without right, the takedown page is the fast door — claims are reviewed within two business days and valid ones removed immediately, with counter-notice available to the poster. Repeat infringement ends accounts.
