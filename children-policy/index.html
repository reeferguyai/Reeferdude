Children & Age Policy: Adults Only, Enforced

This is an adult business and the policy is absolute: the site, its products, its community and its marketing are for adults of legal age in their jurisdiction — 18 as a floor, 21 wherever the law sets it there — and this page states how that line is enforced rather than merely asserted.

The gates

An age gate guards entry, age-restricted products carry their requirement on the page and at checkout, and specified deliveries need an adult signature with ID at the door. Gates are friction on purpose: they are the price of operating this business responsibly, and the shop pays it gladly.

No marketing to minors

Nothing here is designed to appeal to children — not the art direction, not the gamification, not the language. The challenges and badges are adult community features walled behind the same gates. If a piece of our own content ever reads otherwise to you, report it; that feedback is treated as a safety report, not a taste dispute.

Failed checks & families

Failed an age check in error? The appeal is one message to support with the details — false blocks get fixed fast. Parents finding this site in a child's history: the under-age block page exists for a graceful exit, and this policy page is written to be shown, not hidden.
