Strain Guides

Strain guides help you understand what makes one hemp strain different from another. Start with the plant families, learn how terpenes shape aroma and character, and use the guides to narrow down strains that fit your goals and your grow setup.

Indica, sativa, and hybrid families

Strain families describe a plant growth pattern and general character. Indica-leaning plants tend to stay shorter and bushier, sativa-leaning plants tend to grow taller with longer flowering times, and hybrids mix traits from both sides. Families are a starting point, not a guarantee: individual strains vary, and growing conditions matter as much as genetics.

Terpenes shape the experience

Terpenes are the aromatic compounds behind the smell of hemp, from pine and citrus to earth and spice. Common examples include myrcene, limonene, pinene, and caryophyllene. Learning terpenes helps you describe what you like and compare strains with more precision than family labels alone.

How to use these guides

  • Pick a goal first: flavor, growing style, or a specific plant trait.
  • Compare two or three strains side by side instead of chasing a single name.
  • Check the strain index for the full list, then use the strain builder to plan a grow.
