Shipping Policy: How Boxes Move

The shipping policy in full: which carriers, how costs are calculated, the zone timelines, how glass gets packed to survive the journey, and the lost-and-damaged playbook — the operational truth behind the delivery areas page's promises.

Costs & carriers

Shipping is calculated by zone and weight at checkout and shown before payment — the number you see is the number charged. Carriers are the major national networks with tracking at every step; the tracking page reads their scans directly. Free-shipping thresholds, when offered, print on this page and the cart — no surprise conditions.

Packaging glass

Glass ships double-boxed with molded protection — the standard that gets breakage under one percent. If your piece arrives broken anyway, photograph it in the box and the replacement process starts from that photo alone; breakage in transit is our problem, contractually.

Lost & late

A package with no scans for five business days is treated as lost: replacement or refund, your choice, while we chase the carrier. Weather and carrier-network delays extend timelines publicly on the status page rather than quietly in your inbox.
