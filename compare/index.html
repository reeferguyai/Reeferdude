Compare Products: Side By Side, No Spin

Comparison is where marketing dies and facts line up: two or three products, the same spec table, no adjectives. Height, weight, joint size, glass thickness, material, price, warranty — and the honest 'best for' line the crew writes for each.

How to use it

Add products from any category page, or start from a best-sellers pair you are torn between. Specs pull straight from the product pages, so what you compare is exactly what each listing claims.

What specs don't say

Numbers miss hand-feel and draw resistance. That is what the reviews and the product care notes are for — and why every comparison ends with the crew's plain recommendation for different kinds of smokers.
