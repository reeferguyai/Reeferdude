Investors

ReeferDude is a flagship hemp brand with a store engine, a learning platform, and a community. This page gives prospective investors the honest overview: what the business is, how it makes money, and where it is headed.

The business

Two domains work together. ReeferDude is the flagship and demand engine: guides, the AI budtender, community, and brand. PotGrowHub is the commerce engine: shop, marketplace, design tools, and fulfillment. Neither duplicates the other, and each feeds the other.

How it makes money

Revenue comes from store sales, marketplace activity, print-on-demand, membership tiers, consultation, affiliate relationships, advertising, and digital products. No revenue figures are published on this page; prospective investors receive current figures privately after an inquiry.

Where it is headed

The plan is phased: keep the store selling, build the flagship home, then grow the flagship into the primary brand while the store keeps selling. Compliance with the December 11, 2026 federal hemp cap is built into the catalog from the start.
