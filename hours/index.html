Hours & Holiday Schedule: When The Porch Is Open

Clear hours beat vague availability. This page states when humans are on, when the AI budtender covers, and which holidays slow everything down — so you always know whether an answer takes minutes or a day.

The rhythm

Human support: Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 9 PM Eastern, with Report a Problem monitored most often. Ray the AI budtender: always on, genuinely useful for product, strain and grow questions at any hour — and honest about handing off to humans when a question needs one.

Holiday schedule

The shop observes the major US holidays with reduced human hours: New Year's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. Orders (when live) placed on holidays join the next business day's packing queue, stated on the order confirmation itself.

Bench & consultations

In-person bench and consultation work is by appointment within support hours; the consultation page books it. The status page carries any unplanned closures or outages the moment they are known.
