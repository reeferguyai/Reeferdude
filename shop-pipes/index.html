Pipes & Bowls: The Everyday Carry

Pipes are the oldest tool in the kit and still the most reached-for. This category covers spoon pipes, sherlocks, one-hitters and replacement bowls in glass, silicone and wood — pocket pieces, porch pieces and backups for when the good glass is in the wash.

Glass, silicone or wood?

Glass gives the cleanest taste and is easy to clean. Silicone survives drops and travel. Wood is warm in the hand and traditional, but holds flavor over time. Most smokers end up with one of each for different jobs.

Bowl fit matters

Replacement bowls are listed by joint size and gender — match yours before ordering. A 14mm male bowl fits a 14mm female joint; getting it wrong is the most common pipe-accessory mistake, and our size guide page walks through measuring yours.

Care in one line

A pipe cleaned weekly tastes like a new pipe forever. The product care page has the full routine; the short version is warm water, salt, isopropyl, rinse, dry.
