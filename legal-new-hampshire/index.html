New Hampshire: Cannabis Law, Plainly

New Hampshire's cannabis rules, summarised honestly and dated October 2026. This is education, not legal advice — every figure here should be verified against the official source linked in spirit on the fact-check page before you rely on it.

The status

Medical only. New Hampshire runs a medical program and decriminalised small amounts in 2017; recreational legalisation has repeatedly passed one chamber and stalled in the other.

Possession & home grow

Amounts, plant counts and conditions change by amendment and regulation — the status summary above is the shape of the law, not its text. Before acting on any of it, read the current official source for New Hampshire (its cannabis control authority or official code), because a summary can age and a charge cannot be appealed to a webpage.

Travel warning

Nothing crosses state lines legally in the way people casually assume: taking product from a legal state into a prohibition state is trafficking under that state's law, and federal land and aircraft follow federal rules everywhere. The travel rules page is mandatory reading before any trip.
