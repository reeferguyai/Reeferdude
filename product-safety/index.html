Product Safety Notice: Use It Right Or Not At All

Every product family carries real hazards alongside its pleasures, and this page collects the notices in one place: heat and flame, battery safety for vapes, glass integrity, small parts and magnets away from children, and the standing rule that a damaged tool gets retired, not nursed.

Heat, flame & batteries

Torches are tools, not toys — refill and store them like the pressurised fuel they are. Vape batteries: undamaged wraps only, never loose in a pocket with keys, charge on a surface that cannot burn, and retire any battery that runs hot, swells or takes a hard drop. Counterfeit cells are this industry's quiet fire risk; the shop sells none and the quality process tests for them.

Glass & sharps

Inspect glass before each use — a chipped joint or hairline crack retires the piece on the spot; heat plus cracked glass ends in hands. Broken glass is wrapped, labelled and binned, never loose in household trash where it can cut the people who collect it.

Age & access

Everything here is adult equipment: stored away from children and pets, full stop. The children and age policy states the shop's position; this page states the household practice that makes it real.
