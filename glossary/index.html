Glossary

The glossary defines the terms you will meet across ReeferDude, in plain language. If a guide uses a word you do not know, look it up here first. Terms are grouped by topic so you can browse or search for exactly what you need.

Cannabinoids

  • Cannabinoid: a class of compounds found in hemp that interact with the body endocannabinoid system.
  • CBD: cannabidiol, a non-intoxicating cannabinoid commonly found in hemp products.
  • THC: tetrahydrocannabinol, the intoxicating cannabinoid. Hemp products on this site are hemp-derived and must stay within legal limits.
  • Full spectrum: a product type that keeps a range of cannabinoids and terpenes from the plant.

Terpenes and plant terms

  • Terpene: an aromatic compound that gives hemp its smell and contributes to its character.
  • Trichome: the tiny resin glands on hemp flowers where cannabinoids and terpenes concentrate.
  • Decarboxylation: heating hemp flower to convert cannabinoid acids into their active forms.

Growing and product terms

  • Cure: the slow, sealed-container aging step after drying that improves flavor and smoothness.
  • COA: certificate of analysis, a lab report showing what is in a product. See the lab results page.
  • Isolate: a product form containing a single isolated cannabinoid.
