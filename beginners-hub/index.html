Beginner's Hub: Start Here, Start Right

Welcome — everybody starts somewhere, and this hub is the somewhere designed for you. No assumed knowledge, no snobbery, and an honest order to learn things in: what the plant is, how people use it, what the tools do, and how to stay safe and legal while you find out what you like.

The path

Start with Cannabis Basics for the plant itself. Then the dosing guide — start low, go slow is the whole first month. Then gear how-tos for the tool you own, the glossary open in another tab for the slang, and harm reduction before your first session, not after.

First purchases, honestly

You need less than the shop wants to sell you (and this is the shop saying it): one simple pipe or a small bong, a two-piece grinder, papers for backup, and cleaning basics. Everything else is an upgrade you will understand after a month of use. The bundles page has the honest starter kit.

Questions are the point

The budtender chat answers beginner questions at 3 AM without a flicker of judgment, and the FAQ covers the questions everyone asks first. The only bad first-timer move is pretending you know — this plant rewards the humble.
