Unit Converter: Grams, Ounces & Everything Between

Cannabis runs on mixed units: grams and ounces, milliliters and gallons, Fahrenheit and Celsius, EC and PPM, lux and PPFD envy. This converter covers the units this world actually uses, in one place, with the conversions growers get wrong flagged and explained.

The greatest hits

An ounce is 28.35 grams (the street's 28 is close, the scale disagrees). A gallon is 3.785 liters. Nutrient EC and PPM convert on two different scales (500 and 700) — the converter makes you pick yours, because guessing the scale is how plants get burned. Fahrenheit–Celsius for the dry room, inches–centimeters for light distance.

Why a shop hosts this

Because half the mistakes in the troubleshooting guide are unit mistakes wearing a trench coat. One honest converter, linked from every calculator and guide, prevents more ruined grows than any product we could sell.

Precision note

Conversions here use exact factors and sensible rounding for the job — dosing math keeps its decimals, shopping math drops them. The fact-check page holds the factor sources.
