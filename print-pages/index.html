Print-Friendly Pages: The Porch On Paper

Some knowledge belongs on paper: the germination guide taped inside the tent door, the dosing chart on the fridge, the returns policy in the filing cabinet. This page explains the site's print system — every guide and policy carries a print view that strips the decoration and prints the words like it respects your ink.

How it works

Hit print on any content page and the print stylesheet takes over: navigation, banners and sidebars vanish; type goes black-on-white in a readable serif; links print as short references; page breaks fall between sections, not through tables. What you preview is the paper you get.

Best on paper

The dosing guide, germination guide, grow stage checklists, cleaning routines and the legal summaries earn their wall space. The calculators print their results pages with your inputs and the date — a grow journal's best friend.

Paper honesty

Print what you need; the trees in the audience for a hemp site deserve the joke told respectfully. Digital saves — PDFs from the same print view — live just as long and travel better.
