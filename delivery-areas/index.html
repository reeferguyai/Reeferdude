Delivery Areas & Zones: Where We Roll

Delivery is zoned because law and distance both demand it. This page maps the service area plainly: which states and regions get which service, what shipping costs by zone, and the realistic delivery windows — no '3–5 days' fiction for a box crossing the country.

Gear vs restricted items

Plain gear — glass, tools, papers — ships almost everywhere. Anything age-restricted or regulated follows the strictest applicable rule between here and your door, and some items simply do not ship to some places. Each product page states its own restrictions before you check out.

Zones and times

Zone 1 (home region): 1–2 business days. Zone 2 (neighboring states): 2–4. Zone 3 (continental): 3–6. Remote and rural routes can add a day — the tracker shows the carrier's real estimate for your exact address at checkout.
