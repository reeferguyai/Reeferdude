Site Index: Every Page, In Plain Language

Search engines get their sitemap in XML; you get this one: every page on the site, grouped by purpose and described in a plain line each, so the whole porch can be surveyed in one scroll. If a page exists here, it is linked here — and if it is not linked here, it should not exist.

How it is organised

Shop & Orders, Learn & Guides, Culture & Community, Tools, Business & Partners, Company, and Legal & Policy — the same sections you have been browsing, flattened into one map. New pages join this index in the same release that creates them; the index is checked against the site automatically.

Why bother

Big sites hide their best rooms. The strain library's deepest guide, the one calculator that would have saved your grow — an index is how a site respects the reader who wants the whole map instead of the algorithm's slice of it.

The machine version

Crawlers and tools want the XML: it lives at the standard /sitemap.xml address and lists every URL here in machine form, updated in the same breath.
