Stories From The Community: Porch Tales

Data tells you what; stories tell you what it meant. This page collects the community's tales — the first grow that failed beautifully, the grandfather's porch wisdom, the trim-party friendship that outlasted the harvest — edited lightly, credited always, and told in the tellers' own voices.

The standing shelves

First Times (gentle, funny, honest), Grow Diaries (one season, whole arc, photos welcome), Lessons & Losses (the mistakes that teach — the most-read shelf), and Porch Wisdom (the elders' shelf, where the culture keeps its memory).

Tell yours

The submit page takes stories in your own words, long or short. Names can be changed for privacy — say so in the submission — and anything identifying a third party gets their consent or gets cut, no exceptions.

Why stories are infrastructure

Strain databases and legal guides are the shop's skeleton; this page is its memory. Cultures that stop telling their stories become markets. The porch refuses, one tale at a month at a time.
