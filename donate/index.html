Donate

ReeferDude runs on store sales, memberships, and community support. If you want to back the guides and the community directly, this page covers the ways to do it, now and later.

Support right now

  • Join a membership tier to fund guides, tools, and community directly.
  • Share guides and community posts with people who would benefit.
  • Buy through the shop and marketplace, which funds the platform.

Direct donations

Direct donations open later, once the payment setup for contributions is ready. Until then, no donation button on this site collects money, and any page claiming otherwise should be reported through the contact page.

Where support goes

Support funds guide writing, tool development, community events, and platform upkeep. Financial summaries for supporters will be published on the blog when direct donations open.
