Quality & Testing: How Gear Earns Its Shelf

Nothing reaches the catalog on a supplier's say-so. This page documents the testing gauntlet: what gets measured, what gets abused, and what gets rejected — including the rejection rate, because a process that never fails anything is not a process.

The bench gauntlet

Glass is checked for thickness consistency, joint fit and annealing stress under polarized light, then used: filled, pulled, cleaned ten times. Vapes run full battery cycles and temperature accuracy checks. Grinders run a standardized load until the spin tells the truth.

Listing standards

A product page only publishes with verified specs — measured, not copied from a supplier sheet — plus care instructions and a warranty position. If a spec cannot be verified, the listing says so instead of inventing one.

Ongoing audits

Listed products get re-tested on a cycle and whenever a maker changes production. Reader reports feed the queue — a cluster of breakage reports pulls a product back to the bench, and the changelog records the outcome.
