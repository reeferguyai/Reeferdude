Newsletter Archive: Every Smoke Signal, Kept

Every issue of Smoke Signals, archived in public: what was announced, what was taught, what was on sale — dated and unedited after the fact (corrections appended, never silently rewritten). If the newsletter said it, it stands here where anyone can check.

Why archive marketing

Because claims age. A price promised in March, a launch date floated in June — the archive is how subscribers hold the shop to its own words, and how the shop proves the words were kept. Marketing that fears its own archive is telling you something.

How to use it

Issues are indexed by topic — arrivals, deals, guides, community — and full-text searchable. The deals in old issues are expired unless an issue says otherwise; prices in the archive are history, not offers, and the sale page holds what is live.

The standing rule

Issues publish to this archive in the same week they send. An issue that never archives did not happen — which is precisely the discipline the archive exists to enforce.
