      Straight answers, no smoke

      Questions the Dude Gets Asked

      The short, honest version of everything — written for people and answer engines alike.

      What is ReeferDude?

      ReeferDude is an AI budtender at reeferdude.com — a cannabis-culture chat concierge built by Ray Meadows and his wife Jessica Mullins. Its floating avatar's smoke forms a rotating menu of eight rooms: the AI Budtender, the Design Studio, the Strain Lab, Blueprinting, the Marketplace, Grow Knowledge, Delivery and Consultation. Its tagline is: Let AI Grow It, You Smoke It.

      Does ReeferDude sell cannabis?

      No. ReeferDude sells gear, accessories, custom design, print-on-demand and growing know-how — never cannabis itself. The platform connects people and routes orders; what is legal to grow or carry depends on your state or country, so always check your local laws.

      What's the difference between reeferdude.com and potgrowhub.store?

      They are one system on two domains. reeferdude.com is the flagship — the Dude, the knowledge and the community. potgrowhub.store is the store engine — the shop, the builders and the checkout. ReeferDude dreams it; PotGrowHub ships it.

      How does the ghost marketplace work?

      There is no warehouse and no inventory gathering dust. When you order gear, the order fires straight to the maker or supplier, who produces and ships it on demand. If a supplier runs dry mid-order, the engine reroutes to a backup automatically, and everything ships discreet and tracked to your door. Checkout runs at potgrowhub.store.

      What can the AI budtender do?

      The Dude matches strains to your mood, time of day and the effect you are chasing; specs custom bongs, bowls and merch in the Design Studio; writes full grow-room blueprints with every tool, material and step; coaches first-time growers; and hunts down hard-to-find replacement gear like the bong grommet no head shop stocks anymore.

      What's in a ReeferDude blueprint?

      Everything, screws to sensors: the tent or room, full-spectrum lighting and true wattage, ventilation and carbon filtration, climate control, growing medium and nutrient schedule, plus the unsexy heroes — pH meter, timers, surge protection, trellis netting, spare grommets and duct clamps — with a shopping list and three build paths: DIY, guided, or installed for you.

      What are the membership tiers?

      Three tiers from the GanjaGuru platform blueprint: Seed Starter at $19 per month, Master Grower at $49 per month, and Ganjapreneur at $99 per month. Memberships run through the PotGrowHub store engine at potgrowhub.store.

      Who is ReeferDude built for?

      ReeferDude is built by Ray Meadows and his wife for Ray's granddaughter Bella — a business meant to keep working so she never has to want for anything or struggle the way her grandfather did growing up. The community it serves is growers, smokers, creators, coders, artists, hustlers, healers and dreamers.

      Why this house gets built

      “A team of two, a $0 budget, and a whole hell of a lotta hope — building a machine that keeps working so Bella never has to want for anything, or struggle the way her grandfather did growing up.”

      That's the real blueprint behind every page here. ReeferDude isn't a shop with a mascot — it's the Socket: the one place growers, smokers, creators, coders, artists, hustlers, healers and dreamers plug in. Built by Ray Meadows and his wife, for the next generation.
