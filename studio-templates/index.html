Studio Templates: Start From Something Good

Blank canvases intimidate; templates liberate. This gallery holds the Design Studio's starter library — memorial pieces, name-and-date etchings, strain-art trays, band-style tees — each one a finished design you personalise rather than a void you must fill.

How templates work

Pick a template, swap the name, date, strain or photo, adjust colors against the live 3D preview, and order or save. Every template is fully editable down to the fonts — a starting point, not a cage — and the original designer's credit travels with remixes under the Studio's rules.

The collections

Porch Classics (names, dates, places), Strain Art (the library's genetics as graphics), Memorial (for pieces that carry someone with you), and Seasonal (rotating with the challenges). Community templates join the library through the user designs wall, with the maker's opt-in.

Print-ready honesty

Templates are built at true print resolution for their products; the print file help page explains the specs for bringing your own art instead, including the 3D print guide.
