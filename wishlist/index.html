Wishlist: Your Future Porch

The wishlist is your porch-in-planning: every product page has a save button, and everything you save collects here. Share the link when someone asks what you want; keep it private when you are just dreaming with intent.

What it does

Saved items show price changes and restock status at a glance. Bundles containing your saved pieces are flagged, because three wishlist items inside one bundle is the universe giving you a discount.

Demo note

This storefront is a demonstration build with accounts in preview mode, so wishlist saving is shown as a working layout rather than live storage. Your list lives in this browser session in the demo; full accounts arrive with live checkout.

From wishlist to cart

Moving items to the cart keeps their bundle pricing intact. The cart page explains how checkout will work when ordering opens.
