Grow Calculator: Cost It Before You Grow It

Home growing fails financially in one way: surprise. This calculator models the whole first grow — tent, light, ventilation, medium, nutrients, and the electricity nobody budgets — then projects running costs per cycle and a yield range stated as a range, because anyone promising you a number is selling something.

Inputs that matter

Tent size, light wattage (real draw, not marketing watts), your electricity rate, hours per day by stage, and consumable costs per cycle. Defaults are pre-filled with honest mid-range figures, and every default is editable — the calculator shows its assumptions in the open.

Reading the output

First-cycle cost (gear included), per-cycle cost after, cost per gram at three yield scenarios — poor, typical, strong for a grower at your stated experience. If the math says buying beats growing for your situation, the calculator says that too. It works for the truth, not the shop.

Pairs with

The soil, hydro and lighting guide for the choices being costed, the stage guides for the hours being modelled, and the humidity calculator for the drying end of the same grow.
