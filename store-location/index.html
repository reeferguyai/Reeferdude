Store Location & Service Area: Where The Porch Sits

The porch has a real address in the real world: the business is based in Franklin, Ohio, in the southwest of the state between Dayton and Cincinnati. This page sets out what happens here, what the in-person service area covers, and what lives online for everyone else.

Home base

Franklin, Ohio is headquarters — the bench, the studio and the packing table. There is no walk-in retail storefront at this time; the shop is online-first, and this page will carry opening hours and directions the day a physical door opens.

Service area

Online ordering is built to serve the delivery zones on the delivery areas page. In-person services tied to the founding business — device repair bench work and local consultations — serve the Franklin and greater Dayton–Cincinnati corridor by appointment.

Why publish this

Anonymous shops ask for trust they have not earned. A real town, a real service area and a real crew page are the minimum a customer should accept before sending money anywhere — including here.
