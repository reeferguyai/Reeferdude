Product Care & Cleaning: Keep It Tasting New

Every piece you own is slowly becoming a worse version of itself unless it is cleaned — resin dulls flavor, clogs airflow and harbors the funk. This hub collects the care routine for every material in the catalog, in one place.

Glass

Isopropyl alcohol (91%) and coarse salt, shake, soak, rinse warm, dry fully. Weekly for daily pieces. Never boil glass, never use cold water on a hot piece — thermal shock is the silent killer of good bongs.

Silicone, metal and wood

Silicone: hot soapy water or the freezer trick for stuck resin; it can handle what glass cannot. Metal grinders: brush daily, isopropyl soak monthly, never the dishwasher's harsh cycle. Wood: never soak — a dry brush and the occasional food-safe oil wipe.

The 10-minute rule

If cleaning takes more than ten minutes, you waited too long. Set the rhythm — quick rinse after sessions, deep clean weekly — and every piece you own stays at its best with almost no effort.
