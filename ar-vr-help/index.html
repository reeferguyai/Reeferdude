AR & VR Setup Help: Step Into The Porch

The Studio's AR preview puts a piece on your real table; the VR spaces — Chillout VR and the design room — put you inside the porch. This page gets both running: what hardware works, what is optional, and the fixes for the three problems everyone hits first.

What you need

AR works on most modern phones through the browser — no app, no headset. VR works best on standalone headsets (Quest-class) and any WebXR-capable browser setup; a phone in a cardboard viewer gives the taste, not the meal. Everything degrades gracefully to flat 3D on any screen — nobody is locked out of the porch for lack of hardware.

The three classic problems

AR piece floats or sinks: re-scan a lit, textured surface. VR blurry: adjust headset fit before settings — it is fit nine times in ten. Motion discomfort: sit down, use teleport movement, and take the headset off at the first wobble; the Chillout space is designed slow on purpose.

Privacy in headsets

Camera and room data for AR/VR stay on your device in our implementation — the AI and voice data policy covers the details, including voice chat inside VR spaces.
