Cleaning Guides: Every Piece, Every Method

This is the deep end of the product care hub: full cleaning procedures by device, including the rescue protocol for pieces that have not been cleaned since an era best forgotten. Everything here uses cheap, standard supplies — isopropyl, salt, hot water, brushes, patience.

The glass deep-clean

Rinse hot, plug and fill with 91% isopropyl and coarse salt, shake two minutes, soak thirty, brush the stubborn spots, rinse until the water runs clear and tasteless. Percolators need the soak more than the shake. Full procedure with photos-in-words lives on the product care page; this page adds timings by piece size.

Rigs, vapes and grinders

Rigs: warm (never boiling) water loosens reclaim before the alcohol pass — reclaim can be collected or discarded, your call, the method is documented. Vapes: screens and mouthpieces are the flavor bottleneck, alcohol-soak weekly. Grinders: freeze metal grinders for ten minutes and stuck kief brushes right off.

The rescue protocol

For the truly neglected: overnight alcohol soak, pipe cleaners and bottle brushes for the geometry, a second soak, and acceptance that some staining is now character. If a piece still tastes wrong after two full rescues, it has retired — glass is honest that way.
