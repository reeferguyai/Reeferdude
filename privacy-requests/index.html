Privacy Requests: See It, Export It, Delete It

Your data is yours in the only sense that matters: you can see it, take it, and erase it. This page is where those requests live — access (what we hold), export (a copy in a readable file), and deletion (gone, with the few legal exceptions stated plainly).

What we hold

Account details, orders, addresses, wishlist and design saves, and support messages. Not held: card numbers (the payment gateway keeps those), and nothing bought from data brokers, because we buy nothing from data brokers. The data retention page gives the full inventory and clocks.

Timelines, honestly

Access and export requests complete within 30 days, usually much faster. Deletion completes within 30 days except records the law requires keeping (tax and transaction records), which are isolated, never used for marketing, and deleted when their clock runs out.

Demo note

This demonstration storefront holds no live customer data yet; this page publishes the policy and flow the live shop opens with, so the promise predates the data.
