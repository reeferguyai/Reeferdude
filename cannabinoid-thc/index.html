THC: Tetrahydrocannabinol

Tetrahydrocannabinol — THC — is one of the four cannabinoid pages at the core of the learning library. Plain language, sourced claims, no shelf-copy promises.

What it is

THC is the plant's primary intoxicating cannabinoid — the compound behind the high. It binds CB1 receptors in the brain and nervous system, producing euphoria, altered perception, appetite and, at high doses, anxiety or sedation depending on the person and the profile around it.

How it differs

Delta-9 THC is the reference form; delta-8 and other isomers differ in potency and legal treatment. Federal hemp law is being rewritten around total THC: from December 11, 2026, hemp products are capped at 0.4mg total THC per container, and cannabinoids the plant cannot naturally produce lose hemp status on November 12, 2026.

The honest notes

Dose is everything: 2.5–5mg is a beginner edible dose, 10mg a standard serving, and concentrates can deliver 30mg+ in one dab. Tolerance builds fast and resets with breaks. THC impairs driving, interacts with some medications, and is not for adolescents — the developing brain is the one population where the evidence of harm is consistent.
