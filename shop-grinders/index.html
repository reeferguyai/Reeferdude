Grinders: The Unsung Hero

A grinder is the cheapest upgrade in smoking. Evenly ground flower burns evenly, vapes evenly, and stretches further — and a four-piece with a screen quietly collects kief, the most potent part of the plant, in its own chamber.

Two-piece or four-piece?

Two-piece grinders are simple and nearly indestructible — grind and dump. Four-piece adds a storage chamber and a kief screen. If you vape, the finer, fluffier grind of a four-piece is worth it; if you roll on the go, two-piece wins on size.

Metal vs wood

Aircraft-grade aluminum holds a sharp edge for years and cleans easily. Wooden hand grinders are beautiful and gentle but dull faster and should never be soaked. Both are listed with diameter — 50mm is pocket size, 63mm is the porch standard.

Cleaning

A stiff brush after each use, isopropyl soak monthly for metal. A grinder that spins free grinds fine; a gummy one tears. Full routine on the cleaning guides page.
