Wholesale Application: Stock The Porch

Shops, lounges and retailers: this is the door. The wholesale application collects your business details, licence information where applicable, and the lines you want to carry — reviewed by a human within three business days, with pricing tiers explained before you commit to anything.

Who qualifies

Licensed retailers and legitimate resellers with a business registration and, where their jurisdiction requires it for the product class, the relevant licences. Gear-only accounts need less paper than regulated-category accounts — the form asks only for what your chosen lines require.

Tiers, in the open

Tier pricing is volume-based and published to approved applicants in full — no 'call for pricing' games. Opening minimums are modest by design: the program exists to put independent-shop shelves and this catalog in the same ecosystem, not to move containers.

What happens next

Approval brings the wholesale catalogue, terms (payment terms page), and a named human contact. Denials come with the reason and what would change it — the porch does not ghost businesses.
