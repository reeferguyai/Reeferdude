Email Preferences: Your Mail, Your Settings

Between everything and nothing there is a dial, and it belongs to you. This page holds your email preferences: which topics (new arrivals, deals, learning content, community), how often (as it happens, weekly digest, monthly round-up), and the address they go to.

The defaults, honestly

New subscribers start on the weekly digest with all topics — the least annoying configuration that still informs. Nothing here is pre-ticked toward more mail; every increase is a choice you make on this page, reversible in the same visit.

Transactional mail

Order, shipping and security emails are not preference-controlled, by design and by law's expectation — they are the paper trail of your own transactions. Their content is never used for marketing, and they carry no advertising.

Demo note

With accounts in preview mode on this demonstration build, preferences show the production design. The unsubscribe page works as a statement of policy today and a mechanism at launch.
