Lab Results (COAs)

Lab results back product claims with evidence. A certificate of analysis, or COA, is an independent lab report showing cannabinoid content and screening results for a product batch. This page explains how to read one and how batch lookup works.

What a COA shows

  • Cannabinoid profile: measured levels of CBD, THC, and other cannabinoids in the batch.
  • Contaminant screening: results for pesticides, heavy metals, solvents, and microbials where testing applies.
  • Batch match: the batch number on the report should match the batch on the product received.

How to read a COA

Start with the product and batch identifiers, then check the cannabinoid table against the label. A result that matches the label within normal lab variance is a good sign. If a number looks far off, or the batch does not match, contact us before using the product.

Batch lookup

Batch lookup launches with the live store. Until then, COA summaries appear on product pages, and questions about any result go through the contact page with the product and batch details.
