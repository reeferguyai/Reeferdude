Brand Assets: Logos, Colors & The Rules

Everything visual about the brand lives here for the people who legitimately need it: press, partners, affiliates and makers co-branding a drop. Logo files, the color palette, and the short list of do-and-don't rules that keep the porch looking like the porch.

What is in the kit

Primary and stacked logo lockups (SVG and PNG, light and dark), the gold-and-green palette with hex values, and the typography notes. The goddess artwork is brand identity, not clip art — she is licensed for shop use only and is not in the download kit.

The rules

Do not stretch, recolor or re-typeset the logo. Do not place it on anything that implies endorsement of a product we do not sell. Affiliates and partners get a co-brand lockup in the kit that is pre-approved; anything else, ask through media enquiries.

Demo note

Asset downloads activate with the press kit at full launch; this page publishes the rules and inventory now so partners can plan creative against the real system.
