Brands & Makers: Who Made Your Gear

Behind every good piece is a person or a small shop that made it. This page indexes the makers in the catalog — independent glassblowers, small workshops and established gear brands — with notes on their style, their standards and the pieces of theirs we carry.

Why makers matter

Mass-produced glass is fine until it isn't — thin spots, sloppy joints, annealing shortcuts you cannot see until the piece fails. Named makers stake their reputation on every piece, and that accountability is worth paying for.

Independent first

The marketplace side of the shop is built for independent makers: glassblowers and gear creators listing their own work. The Maker Marketplace page explains how makers join and what the seller terms promise them.

Shop by maker

Each maker's pieces carry their story on the product page — who made it, where, and how. If a piece has no maker story, it is a workshop staple and is labelled that way.
