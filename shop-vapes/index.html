Vapes & Dabs: Heat, Not Burn

Vaporising instead of burning changes everything: more flavor, less harshness, and leftovers you can actually use. This category covers dry-herb vapes, dab rigs, bangers and tools — with the temperature guidance that makes the difference between a great session and a scorched one.

Dry herb vs concentrate

Dry-herb vapes heat ground flower below combustion (roughly 350–430°F) so terpenes vaporise without smoke. Dab rigs vaporise concentrates on a hot quartz or titanium surface. The gear overlaps less than people think — buy for what you actually use.

Temperature is the whole game

Low temps (under 360°F) are all flavor and light effect; higher temps bring the heavy cannabinoids and thicker clouds. A vape with real temperature control lets you walk that ladder in one bowl instead of guessing.
