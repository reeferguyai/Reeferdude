Veg Stage: Build The Frame

Veg is where the plant builds the frame that flower will fill: branches, roots and sheer size. How long you veg, how you train, and how you feed now decides the harvest ceiling later — flower only decorates the structure veg built.

Light and time

18 hours light, 6 dark is the standard rhythm; the plant grows until you flip the schedule. Indoors, veg length is your size dial — every extra week adds yield and adds weeks. Plan for the stretch: most strains grow 50–100% taller in early flower.

Training toolkit

Topping (cutting the main tip to split the top), LST (tying branches sideways for an even canopy), and ScrOG nets all pursue the same goal — every bud site at the same distance from the light. Pick one method per grow while learning; the troubleshooting guide covers the recovery when a branch snaps.

Feeding

Veg wants nitrogen-forward feeding and steady calcium/magnesium in most water sources. Feed at half the bottle's stated strength until the plant proves it wants more — deficiencies show slowly, burns show fast, and the plant-health pages teach the leaf-reading for both.
