Bundles & Starter Kits: One Box, Whole Porch

A bundle is a decision already made well: the bong with the right bowl, the grinder that matches the tray, the cleaning kit sized to the piece. Starter kits are built for someone setting up from nothing; upgrade bundles are built for someone whose porch is already rolling.

The starter logic

Every starter kit contains exactly what a first setup needs and nothing it does not: piece, grinder, papers or screens, and cleaning basics. No filler, no novelty items padding the price.

Price honesty

Each bundle lists what its parts cost separately next to the bundle price. The saving is real arithmetic, not a crossed-out imaginary number — that practice is banned here by the terms of sale.

Build your own

Want a different combination? The price and bundle builder lets you assemble your own kit from the catalog and shows the bundled price live as you add pieces.
