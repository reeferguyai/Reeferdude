Challenges & Badges: Earn Your Porch Stripes

Challenges give the porch its game: monthly prompts (grow a legal herb garden alongside your reading, photograph glass in sunlight, finish the germination guide's quiz) and standing learning tracks (Grow School, Strain Scholar, Glass Master) — each completion earning badges that live on your profile and XP on the boards.

How badges work

Badges mark verified completion: finish the track's pages and its quiz, and the badge is yours permanently, season resets do not touch it. Some badges are community-verified (photo challenges judged by vote), most are knowledge-verified — all are listed here with their exact requirements before you start.

This month's challenge

The current monthly challenge and its leaderboard live at the top of this page once accounts are fully live. In the demonstration build, the full challenge catalogue and badge cabinet are published so the game can be inspected end to end.

Difficulty honesty

Tracks are labelled by real effort: an evening, a week, a season. A badge that takes a growing season says so — the porch does not sell grind disguised as fun.
