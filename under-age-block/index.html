Under-Age Block: This Porch Is For Grown Folks

This page is the other side of the age gate: if you are under the legal age for this site where you live, the porch is closed to you — kindly, completely, and without a workaround we will help with. The rest of the internet is enormous; this corner of it is for adults, by law and by choice.

Why so firm

The products, content and community here are adult by nature and by statute. The gate and this block are how the business keeps its licence, its conscience and its community's trust — they are not a puzzle to solve, and trying to defeat them ends accounts.

If this is a mistake

Adults blocked in error — a mistyped birthdate, a shared device — can reach support through the contact page with the details. False blocks are fixed quickly and without suspicion; the gate errs on the side of asking, and humans sort the rest.

For parents

Found this in a family browsing history? The children and age policy page explains the site's full position and enforcement, written to be shown to another adult without embarrassment on either side.
