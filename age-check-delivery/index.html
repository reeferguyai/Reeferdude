Age Check At Delivery: Why The Driver Asks

Some deliveries arrive with a knock and a request: an adult of legal age must sign, and the driver may check ID. This page explains which orders trigger it, what counts as ID, and what happens if nobody eligible is home — so delivery day holds no surprises.

When it applies

Age-restricted products and orders above certain values ship adult-signature-required. The requirement is set by law in many places and by carrier policy in others; either way it is flagged on the product page and again at checkout, never sprung on you after.

Making it smooth

Have a government photo ID ready and be the person who ordered where possible. If daytime delivery never works, carriers can hold the box at a nearby depot for pickup with the same ID — the tracking page offers that option once the box is moving.

No exceptions, no attitude

Drivers cannot waive the check and neither can we. It protects the shop's licence to operate and keeps restricted products out of the wrong hands — the same standard as the age gate on this site.
