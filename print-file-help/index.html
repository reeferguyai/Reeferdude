Print File Help: Get Your Art Print-Ready

Great art dies on bad files. This page is the complete spec sheet: what the Studio accepts for trays, etchings, apparel and stickers (formats, resolution, color mode, bleed), and the 3D printing guide for the digital download products — printers, materials, settings and safety.

2D specs, plainly

Trays and stickers: PNG or SVG, 300 DPI at print size, 3mm bleed, RGB accepted (we convert and proof). Etching: vector strongly preferred — lines etch, gradients suggest. Apparel: PNG with transparency or vector, sized to the garment's print area shown in the Studio preview.

3D print guide

Digital products download as STL and 3MF with a settings card: PLA or PETG recommended, layer heights and infill per product, and the safety line — printed pieces are for dry use and display unless the listing certifies a material otherwise; food and smoke-path safety is stated per file, never assumed.

When in doubt

Upload what you have and the Studio's pre-flight flags problems before you pay for anything — resolution warnings, transparency issues, text too small to etch. A flagged file is a conversation, not a rejection.
