User-Submitted Designs: The Community Studio Wall

The Studio wall: designs the community made with the Design Studio tools — custom trays, etched-glass art, label and sticker work — displayed with their makers' names and, where the maker allows, available as the base for your own custom order.

Ownership, in lights

Every design here belongs to its maker. Displaying is licensed, selling is not: if a design is available as a custom-order base, the maker opted in and earns from every piece — the terms are on the intellectual property policy page, and they are written to be read by humans.

Get on the wall

Finish a design in the Studio and submit it from your saved designs gallery. Curated weekly; the standard is effort and originality, not professional polish — first attempts sit next to masterpieces here, labelled with love as both.

Remix rules

Where a maker allows remixing, the Studio forks their design with credit attached permanently in the file's history. Where they do not, the design is view-only. The setting is the maker's, chosen at submission, changeable at will.
