Quizzes: Know Your Leaf

The quiz porch: short, honest tests that teach while they grade. Strain identification, grow-room basics, terpene matching, history — each quiz ends with the answers explained and links into the guides, so a wrong answer becomes a read chapter instead of a dead end.

The standing quizzes

Strain Savant (name the genetics from the description), Tent Tech (grow environment basics), Terpene Match (aroma to effect), Porch Historian (the timeline in ten questions), and Glass Class (gear knowledge for buyers). Scores feed the challenges and badges system for members who want them to.

No gotchas

Questions test what the site teaches — every answer is findable in the guides, and the results page shows exactly where. A quiz you cannot pass after reading is a quiz we wrote badly, and the feedback link on each results page is read by the person who wrote it.

The big one

The strain finder quiz is its own tool — five questions that end in a strain recommendation, not a score. It lives on its own page and feeds the strain match by mood tool for returning visitors.
