Membership

ReeferDude membership unlocks deeper learning, tools, and community. Three tiers cover everyone from first-time growers to people building a hemp business. Billing, renewal, and cancellation terms are on the subscription terms page.

Seed Starter, 19 dollars per month

Seed Starter is the entry tier for new growers. It covers the core guides, community access, and the basic tools you need to plan and track a grow without getting overwhelmed.

Master Grower, 49 dollars per month

Master Grower adds deeper courses, advanced planning tools, and priority help for growers who want to level up. It suits growers who are past the basics and want structured learning and feedback.

Ganjapreneur, 99 dollars per month

Ganjapreneur is the top tier for people building something: a brand, a shop, or a content channel. It covers business-focused guidance, marketplace and design tools, and the full community and learning library.
