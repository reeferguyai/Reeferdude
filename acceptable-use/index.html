Acceptable Use: The Site's House Rules

Acceptable use is the agreement under every visit: the site, its tools, its content and its AI budtender are offered for lawful, personal and (for partners) agreed commercial use — and this page draws the boundary lines in plain language.

The may-nots

No automated scraping that burdens the service, no probing or attacking the site's security (good-faith research goes through security.txt and is welcomed there), no impersonation, no uploading of others' work as your own, no use of the budtender or tools to facilitate illegal activity, and no attempt to make the AI misrepresent itself as human or as a medical professional.

Accounts & content

One person, one account, real information. Posted content must be yours or licensed, lawful, and within the community rules — which are part of this policy by reference for all community spaces.

Enforcement

Violations meet proportionate response: warning, feature limits, account closure for the serious or repeated — with the reason stated and an appeal route through contact. The policy changes prospectively with notice on the changelog; continued use after notice is acceptance, as is standard and as is stated here in the open.
