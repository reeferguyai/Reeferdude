Bongs: Glass Worth Rollin'

A good bong is the centerpiece of the porch. This category gathers every bong in the catalog — classic beakers, percolator pieces and custom work — each listed with its height, joint size, glass thickness and care needs, so you know exactly what you are getting before it ships.

How to choose

Start with size: tabletop pieces in the 8–14 inch range cool smoke well without being fragile. Check the joint size (14mm and 18mm are the standards) so your bowls and accessories match, and look for glass at least 5mm thick if the piece will travel or get shared.

Percolator or straight tube?

Percolators add diffusion — more bubbles, smoother pull, more cleaning. A straight tube or beaker is simpler, sturdier and easier to keep spotless. Neither is wrong; it is about how much ritual you want in your session and your cleaning routine.
