Founders / Team

ReeferDude is built by a small team with a clear motivation. Meet the founders behind the flagship, the store engine, and the community.

Ray Meadows

Ray is a longtime technician who has repaired computers, laptops, and devices for years, and a natural seller with instincts going back to childhood. After major surgery in 2019, he taught himself technology and turned that learning into building. He leads the technical and product work behind ReeferDude.

Jessica Mullins

Jessica is Ray's wife and co-founder, and the two lead the venture together. The About page tells the joint story; this page records the team as it stands: two founders, building in the open, with community help.

Why this team, why now

The team is small on purpose: fewer layers between a grower question and a real answer. Growth comes from guides that help, tools that work, and a community that trusts the brand, not from headcount.
