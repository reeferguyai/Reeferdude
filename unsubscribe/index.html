Unsubscribe: Leave The Smoke Signals

Leaving should be as easy as joining was. This page unsubscribes your email from the Smoke Signals newsletter immediately — no login, no survey, no 'are you sure' maze, no retention specialist in a chat window. One action, confirmed on this page, done.

What stops and what does not

Newsletter marketing stops at once. Transactional mail — order confirmations, shipping notices, account security — continues while you hold an account, because those are receipts, not marketing; closing the account ends everything but the legally retained records described in data retention.

Changed your mind

Re-subscribing is the same one step on the newsletter page, and you will be welcomed without a single remark about the interval. Preferences finer than all-or-nothing — frequency, topics — live on the email preferences page.

Feedback, optionally

If a specific thing drove you off — too often, off-topic, a broken link — the optional one-line box on this page exists and is read. Optional means optional: the unsubscribe completes whether or not you ever touch it.
