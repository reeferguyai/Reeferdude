Saved Designs: Your Studio Shelf

The Studio saves your work the way a porch shelf holds pieces in progress: drafts, finished designs, ordered pieces and their re-order buttons, all in one gallery tied to your account. Start on your phone, finish on your laptop, order when it is right.

What lives here

Drafts (auto-saved as you work), finals (locked versions as ordered — re-orderable exactly), and your submissions to the community wall with their status. Design files export in print-ready formats from any final: your work, your files, no hostage-taking.

Demo note

Accounts on this demonstration storefront run in preview mode, so the gallery shows the production layout with example designs marked as examples. Live saving arrives with live accounts.

Sharing & remixing

Each design carries your chosen sharing setting — private, view-only wall, or remixable with credit. Changing it later changes it everywhere, including pieces already on the wall; the setting is yours because the work is.
