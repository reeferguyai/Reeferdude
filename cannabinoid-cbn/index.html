CBN: Cannabinol

Cannabinol — CBN — is one of the four cannabinoid pages at the core of the learning library. Plain language, sourced claims, no shelf-copy promises.

What it is

CBN is what THC becomes with time, heat and air — the aged-cannabis cannabinoid. It is mildly intoxicating at most, and its reputation is sleep: CBN products dominate the 'night' shelf of the hemp market.

How it differs

The sleep evidence is honestly thinner than the marketing: early research and a mountain of anecdote, with the sedative effect often traced partly to aged terpenes traveling with it. CBN is best understood as promising and unproven — which this site grades openly rather than repeating shelf copy.

The honest notes

Because CBN comes from THC degradation, it sits in the same tightening legal frame: products are being reformulated against the December 2026 total-THC caps, and converted or synthetic versions face the November 2026 classification change. Check the legal guide for the current state of any CBN product.
