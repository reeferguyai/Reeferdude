Compliance

Hemp rules change, and ReeferDude follows them closely. This page summarizes the rules that shape what the store sells, how products are described, and who can buy. It is general information, not legal advice.

The December 11, 2026 federal cap

Starting December 11, 2026, federal law caps hemp products at 0.4 mg of total THC per container. The store designs its catalog around that cap: products are formulated, tested, and labeled to stay within it, and anything that cannot comply is not sold.

What the cap means for shoppers

Product labels show total THC content so you can see compliance directly, and lab results back the label where testing applies. If a product listing ever looks inconsistent with the cap, report it through the contact page and it will be pulled pending review.

State and local rules

Some states and localities add their own hemp rules, including age limits and product restrictions. The store follows the stricter rule where a destination requires it, and checkout confirms eligibility before an order is accepted.
