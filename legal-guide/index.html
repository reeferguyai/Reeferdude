Legal Guide: Country, State & Region Rules

Cannabis law is a patchwork that changes yearly, sometimes monthly. This hub organises it: one page per US state, summaries for major countries and regions, and — most importantly — the method for verifying the current rule where you actually live, because this page can age and the law will not wait for our edits.

United States

Federal law still lists cannabis as a controlled substance while most states run medical or adult-use programs — the tension is real and unresolved. Each state page in this guide gives that state's status (adult-use, medical, CBD-limited or prohibited), possession framing and home-grow position, dated and disclaimed.

Beyond the US

Canada and Uruguay run full adult-use systems; Germany's 2024 law legalised possession and home grow with cannabis clubs; many European states decriminalise small amounts while prohibiting sale. The travel rules page is mandatory reading before crossing any border with any product — borders are where the strictest rules on earth live.

Verify, always

Nothing here is legal advice. Every page carries its check date and the official source to confirm against — your state's cannabis control board or official code. When this guide and the official source disagree, the official source is right and we want the correction through Report a Problem.
