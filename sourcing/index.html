Sourcing & Suppliers: Where It All Comes From

A catalog is a supply chain wearing a shopfront. This page opens ours up: where products originate, the standards suppliers must meet, and the preference order — independent maker, small workshop, regional distributor, and only then the big catalogs.

The preference order

Maker-direct comes first because accountability is personal and the money stays closer to the hands that made the thing. Every tier down trades a little of that away, so every tier down has to justify itself on quality or price honesty.

Supplier standards

Accurate specs, consistent production, honest materials (borosilicate means borosilicate), and packaging that is not a landfill starter kit. Suppliers are re-scored yearly; two failed audits in a row ends the relationship, and delisted suppliers are noted in the changelog.

Become a supplier

Makers and suppliers apply through the supplier application — samples go through the same bench gauntlet as everything else, no exceptions for friends of the porch.
