Offline Notice: You're Off The Grid (Briefly)

No signal, no problem — up to a point. This notice appears when your connection drops: pages you have already visited stay readable from your device's cache, your cart and design drafts are held locally, and everything syncs when you reconnect. The porch has a memory; it just cannot fetch new wood right now.

What still works

Recently read guides and pages: fully. The calculators: yes, they run on your device. Your wishlist and saved designs: viewable, editable, queued to sync. Checkout and anything needing the live catalog: paused until you are back — the site will say so rather than failing silently.

What to do

Check your connection, wander back into coverage, refresh. If the site itself is down (different problem, ours not yours), the status page — hosted separately for exactly this reason — carries the word.

Why this page exists

Because 'no internet' and 'site broken' feel identical at 1 AM, and a shop that respects you labels its own failures separately from yours. This distinction, maintained honestly, is the whole point of the status system.
