Payment Terms: Money Rules, In Writing

The money rules, complete: when your card is charged, how authorisations work, refund timelines, what a chargeback does, and how the shop handles the disputes nobody wants — all published before you ever check out.

Charging

Cards are charged at order acceptance, not at cart. Authorisation holds that do not convert release on the gateway's schedule (typically 3–7 days, bank-dependent). Failed payments leave the order unpaid and unshipped — never half-processed in silence; the confirmation email states the payment status on every order.

Refunds

Approved refunds return to the original payment method: initiated within 2 business days of approval, landing in 5–10 business days depending on your bank. Store credit, when you choose it, is instant and never expires — but it is always your choice, never the default we slip you.

Chargebacks & disputes

Talk to us first is a cliché because it works: most chargebacks trace to a fixable problem, and the support route resolves faster than a bank dispute. Fraudulent chargebacks are contested with the order record; honest ones are refunded and studied — a pattern of them is a bug in our shop, and we treat it that way.
