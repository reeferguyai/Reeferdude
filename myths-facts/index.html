Myths & Facts: Porch Wisdom, Audited

Cannabis culture runs on inherited claims, some gold and some garbage. This page takes the twenty most repeated ones and grades each: true, mostly true, half-true, or folklore — with the reasoning shown and the fact-check page holding the receipts.

The greatest hits

'Indica = sleepy, sativa = energetic': half-true — effects track terpene and cannabinoid profiles more than the label, and modern hybrids blur the families anyway. 'Holding hits longer gets you higher': folklore — absorption is near-instant; you are mostly oxygen-starving. 'More expensive = better': folklore with a marketing department.

The serious ones

'It's natural so it's safe': false as stated — natural says nothing about dose or interaction; the harm reduction page exists precisely because respect beats slogans. 'You can't overdo edibles': dangerously false — you cannot fatally overdose, but a 200mg edible eaten in impatience is a legendary bad night; the dosing guide prevents it.

How we grade

Every grade here links its evidence class: controlled study, survey data, industry consensus or porch tradition labelled as such. Disagree with a grade? The fact-check page takes challenges with sources — corrections get published, with credit.
